# Answer

Using [Euler-Cauchy equation](../order_two_linear.md#euler-cauchy-equations), we know that $x^{m}$ is a solution. Substituiting and taking out the common factor $x^{m}$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
    m^{2} - 4m + 4 &= 0\newline
    \implies m &= 2
\end{aligned}
$$


Since this is the case of double root, the general solution is

$$
\begin{aligned}
    y = \roundbr{c_{1} + c_{2}\ln x}x^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$
